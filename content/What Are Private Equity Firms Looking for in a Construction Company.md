As construction company owners approach retirement and consider selling their businesses, private equity firms have emerged as an increasingly viable acquisition option. However, owners should understand what these firms prioritize during acquisition evaluations.

Financial performance is a critical factor in these transactions. According to Construction Pros, companies can strengthen their appeal through several key strategies:

  - Anticipate and answer scrutiny. Private equity firms excel at spotting issues. Establishing robust financial processes early prevents last-minute scrambling and reduces the risk of overlooking significant details when buyer interest materializes.
  - Ease integration fears. Buyers gain confidence when companies demonstrate current technology infrastructure, skilled teams and a commitment to industry standards. During negotiations, timely information sharing, honest communication and transparent capability discussions build trust.
  - Enhance the quality of your Quality of Earnings Report. Developing reliable monthly financial summaries is essential. This involves reconstructing work-in-progress reporting, optimizing asset policies, eliminating expense errors and producing consistent forecasts so cost misclassification doesn't undermine valuations.
