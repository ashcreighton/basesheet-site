According to Microsoft's 2025 Work Trend Index, 80% of global workers say they don't have enough time or energy to do their work, largely because of constant interruptions. *Fast Company* outlines five strategies for fitting in breaks despite time constraints.

#### Turn dead time into connection time
Use meeting setup moments for recovery and social interaction rather than responding to emails.

#### Reset your nervous system
Combat the body's fight-or-flight response triggered by work urgency through nasal breathing exercises — a five-second inhale and exhale cycle repeated for 60 to 90 seconds.

#### Let yourself focus
Eliminate multitasking and phone use during meetings to preserve cognitive capacity and attention.

#### Clear your brain, then let it wander
Write down unfinished tasks and take walks to allow room for problem-solving and creative thinking during mental breaks.

#### Align to what you need
Choose breaks that match your specific needs, and avoid activities like social media scrolling that prove draining rather than restorative.
